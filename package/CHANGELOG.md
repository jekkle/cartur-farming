# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release. On the game's own Farming skill: faster growth, bigger harvests, growth
  progress, any-biome crops and cheaper planting. Fields of any size up to 10 x 10 with a preview,
  crop snapping, replant on harvest, and group harvest for crops and beehives.
  Inspired by Smoothbrain's Farming and Advize's PlantEasily.
